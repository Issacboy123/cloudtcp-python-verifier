# MUTCD Part 6 Rules Extraction (TCP-W1-1)

### SIGN-01 — GUIDANCE — Advance warning spacing (Table 6B-1)

Minimum spacing between advance warning signs by road type (Table 6B-1). A = transition/point of restriction to first sign; B = first to second sign; C = second to third sign. 'First sign' is the one closest to the TTC zone. Values are approximate and for guidance only.

**Citation:** Table 6B-1; Sec. 6B.04 ¶07, p. 773

### SIGN-02 — GUIDANCE — Urban nearest warning sign 4S-8S

On urban streets the nearest warning sign to the TTC zone should sit 4 to 8 x speed limit (mph) feet from it, using the high end when speeds are relatively high.

**Citation:** Sec. 6B.04 ¶04, p. 771

### SIGN-03 — GUIDANCE — Rural first warning 8S-12S and 1500 ft open highway

On rural highways the first warning sign should sit 8 to 12 x speed limit (mph) feet from the TTC zone; the advance warning area should extend 1,500 ft or more on open highway.

**Citation:** Sec. 6B.04 ¶06, p. 771

### SIGN-04 — OPTION — Single-sign advance area 100 ft

With a single advance warning sign (e.g., low-speed residential street) the advance warning area may be as short as 100 ft.

**Citation:** Sec. 6B.04 ¶05, p. 771

### SIGN-05 — GUIDANCE — Freeway advance 1/2 mile

Freeway/expressway advance warning should extend as far as 1/2 mile or more.

**Citation:** Sec. 6B.04 ¶03, p. 771

### SIGN-06 — GUIDANCE — Table 6B-1 field adjustments

Table 6B-1 distances should be adjusted for field conditions (increase for reaction time; decrease to put a sign just downstream of an intersection/major driveway). Advance warning may be omitted if the activity area is far enough from the road users' path.

**Citation:** Sec. 6B.04 ¶07-09, p. 771

### SIGN-07 — GUIDANCE — Closest sign 100 ft urban to 1000+ freeway

Where a series of two or more advance warning signs is used, the closest sign to the transition/activity area should be about 100 ft (low-speed urban) up to 1,000 ft or more (freeway/expressway).

**Citation:** Sec. 6H.02 ¶01, p. 798

### SIGN-08 — GUIDANCE — ROAD WORK AHEAD first upstream

When multiple advance warning signs are used, ROAD WORK AHEAD (W20-1) should be the first sign road users encounter (the furthest upstream).

**Citation:** Sec. 6H.02 ¶02, p. 798

### SIGN-09 — GUIDANCE — Crossroad/driveway warning

Where traffic can enter the TTC zone from a crossroad or major (high-volume) driveway, an advance warning sign should be used on that crossroad/driveway.

**Citation:** Sec. 6H.03 ¶02, p. 802

### SIGN-10 — GUIDANCE — ROAD WORK on work road

ROAD WORK (W20-1) should be located in advance of the work space or any detour, on the road where work is taking place.

**Citation:** Sec. 6H.03 ¶01, p. 802

### SIGN-11 — GUIDANCE — Signs on right-hand side

Signs should be on the right-hand side of the roadway unless otherwise provided.

**Citation:** Sec. 6F.02 ¶01, p. 789

### SIGN-12 — OPTION — Both sides / portable / barricade mounting

For special emphasis, signs may be placed on both left and right sides; signs on portable supports may be placed within the roadway; signs may be mounted on or above barricades.

**Citation:** Sec. 6F.02 ¶02, p. 789

### SIGN-13 — STANDARD — Rural mounting height 5 ft

Rural roadside signs: bottom of sign at least 5 ft above the near edge of pavement.

**Citation:** Sec. 6F.02 ¶04; Fig. 6F-1A, p. 789

### SIGN-14 — STANDARD — Urban/sidewalk mounting height 7 ft

Business/commercial/residential areas where parking or pedestrian movement is likely or view may be obstructed: bottom of sign at least 7 ft above top of curb (or near edge of traveled way if no curb). Signs over sidewalks: at least 7 ft above the sidewalk.

**Citation:** Sec. 6F.02 ¶05-06; Fig. 6F-1C,D, p. 790

### SIGN-15 — STANDARD — Portable support 1 ft; secondary -1 ft option

Sign on a barricade or other portable support: bottom at least 1 ft above the traveled way. A secondary sign mounted below another may be 1 ft lower than the heights in SIGN-13/14 (Option).

**Citation:** Sec. 6F.02 ¶07-08, p. 790

### SIGN-16 — GUIDANCE — Lateral offsets Fig 6F-1

Lateral offset per Figure 6F-1: rural roadside 6 to 12 ft from the pavement edge; business/commercial/residential with curb, at least 2 ft from the curb face; without curb, 6 to 12 ft. Figure B (rural, with advisory speed plaque) shows a 6 ft minimum and 4 ft minimum plaque height.

**Citation:** Fig. 6F-1 (Sec. 6F.02), p. 790

### SIGN-17 — GUIDANCE — No supports on sidewalks/bike; ADA

Neither portable nor permanent sign supports should be placed on sidewalks, bicycle facilities, or areas designated for pedestrians/bicyclists. Signs must be placed per ADA (2010 ADA Standards Sec. 307).

**Citation:** Sec. 6F.02 ¶09-10, p. 790

### SIGN-18 — GUIDANCE — Portable supports max 3 days (exceptions)

Signs on portable supports that do not meet Part 2 minimum mounting heights should not stay more than 3 days, except R9-8 to R9-11a, R11 series, W1-6 to W1-8 series, M4-10, E5-1 and similar (Option).

**Citation:** Sec. 6F.02 ¶11-12, p. 790-791

### SIGN-19 — GUIDANCE — Barricade rail coverage; 7 ft for large signs STANDARD part

Signs on Type 3 barricades should cover no more than 50% of the top two rails or 33% of the total area of the three rails. Large signs over 50 sq ft on multiple breakaway posts: at least 7 ft ground clearance (Standard).

**Citation:** Sec. 6F.02 ¶14-15, p. 791

### SIGN-20 — STANDARD — Lane Closed signs W20-5/5a

A Lane(s) Closed sign shall be used in advance of the point where one or more through lanes of a multi-lane road are closed. Single lane: RIGHT (LEFT) LANE CLOSED (W20-5); two or more adjacent lanes: XX RIGHT (LEFT) LANES CLOSED (W20-5a).

**Citation:** Sec. 6H.07 ¶01-02, p. 805

### SIGN-21 — GUIDANCE — Flagger W20-7 advance

Flagger (W20-7) sign should be used in advance of any point where a flagger is stationed. BE PREPARED TO STOP (W3-4) may be added; in TA-10 it goes between the Flagger and ONE LANE ROAD signs, in TA-13 before the Flagger symbol sign.

**Citation:** Sec. 6H.15 ¶01-02; TA-10 note 8; TA-13 note 7, p. 806, 878, 884

### SIGN-22 — STANDARD — ONE LANE ROAD W20-4 only for shared lane

ONE LANE ROAD (W20-4) shall be used only in advance of the point where traffic in both directions must share a single lane.

**Citation:** Sec. 6H.06 ¶01, p. 805

### SIGN-23 — GUIDANCE — ROAD CLOSED W20-3; R11-2 on Type 3

ROAD (STREET) CLOSED (W20-3) should be used in advance of a closure to all road users (or all but local). R11-2 series should be centered on or above a Type 3 barricade at the closure and shall not be used where traffic continues through a reduced-lane zone or where the closure is some distance beyond the sign.

**Citation:** Sec. 6H.05 ¶01; Sec. 6G.04 ¶01-04, p. 793, 805

### SIGN-24 — STANDARD — Shoulder Work legends; freeway sequence GUIDANCE part

Shoulder Work signs carry one of the legends SHOULDER WORK (W21-5), RIGHT (LEFT) SHOULDER CLOSED (W21-5a), or ... XX FT/AHEAD (W21-5b). On freeways/expressways W21-5b then W21-5a should precede the shoulder work and be preceded by ROAD WORK AHEAD (Guidance).

**Citation:** Sec. 6H.22 ¶02-04, p. 807

### SIGN-25 — GUIDANCE — END ROAD WORK G20-2

END ROAD WORK (G20-2), when used, should be near the downstream end of the termination area (engineering judgment). May be on the back of a warning sign facing the other way or on a Type 3 barricade.

**Citation:** Sec. 6H.36 ¶01-02, p. 810

### SIGN-26 — GUIDANCE — ROAD WORK NEXT XX MILES >2 mi; whole mile STANDARD

ROAD WORK NEXT XX MILES (G20-1) should be installed in advance of TTC zones longer than 2 miles; distance stated to the nearest whole mile (Standard).

**Citation:** Sec. 6H.35 ¶01-03, p. 810

### SIGN-27 — GUIDANCE — Pedestrian SIDEWALK CLOSED R9 series

SIDEWALK CLOSED (R9-9) at the beginning of the closed sidewalk, at intersections preceding it, and along it as needed; R9-10 at the start when a parallel sidewalk exists across the road; R9-11 to direct pedestrians to open crosswalks; R9-11a just beyond where pedestrians are redirected.

**Citation:** Sec. 6G.10 ¶02-05, p. 797

### SIGN-28 — STANDARD — Audible devices with SIDEWALK CLOSED CROSS HERE

SIDEWALK CLOSED CROSS HERE signs shall include audible information devices; audible devices shall be provided where midblock closures/changed crosswalks leave visually-impaired pedestrians without adequate communication.

**Citation:** TA-28 note 5-6; TA-29 note 3-4, p. 914, 916

### SIGN-29 — STANDARD — Crosswalk closure no parking 50 ft

Crosswalk closure: curb parking shall be prohibited for at least 50 ft in advance of the midblock crosswalk.

**Citation:** TA-29 note 2, p. 916

### SIGN-30 — STANDARD — Advisory plaques only with warning signs

Advisory Speed plaque (W13-1P) may only be used with a warning sign, never alone; at least 24x24 in when used with a sign 36x36 in or larger; do not mount until agency determines the speed. NEXT XX MILES plaque (W7-3aP) likewise only with a warning sign, at least 30x24 in on signs 36x36 or larger.

**Citation:** Sec. 6H.32 ¶02; Sec. 6H.33 ¶03, p. 809-810

### SIGN-31 — STANDARD — TTC warning sign sizes Table 6H-1

TTC warning signs use the sizes in Table 6H-1 (conventional road / freeway-expressway / minimum). Core signs: W20-1, W20-3, W20-4, W20-5/5a, W20-7, W4-2, W3-4, W21-5 series are 36x36 (conventional), 48x48 (freeway/expressway), 30x30 (minimum). Regulatory: R11-2 series 48x30; R9-9 24x12; R9-10 24x12; R9-11 24x18; R9-11a 24x12; Stop on paddle 18x18 (Table 6G-1). G20-1/G20-2 36x18 conventional, 48x24 freeway.

**Citation:** Table 6H-1; Table 6G-1, p. 796, 803-804

### SHLD-02 — OPTION — Short duration 60 min may eliminate signs if vehicle lights

For short-duration operations of 60 minutes or less (TA-3), all signs and channelizing devices may be eliminated if a vehicle with activated high-intensity rotating/flashing/strobe lights is used. Hazard flashers may supplement but shall not replace those lights.

**Citation:** TA-3 note 5-7, p. 864

### SHLD-03 — OPTION — TA-1 ROAD WORK AHEAD omit behind barrier/24in curb/15ft

TA-1: ROAD WORK AHEAD may be omitted where the work space is behind a barrier, more than 24 in behind the curb, or 15 ft or more from the edge of any roadway.

**Citation:** TA-1 note 3, p. 860

### TAPER-01 — GUIDANCE — Taper length L formulas

Taper length L: speed 40 mph or less: L = WS^2/60; speed 45 mph or more: L = WS. W = offset width in ft; S = posted speed, or off-peak 85th-percentile speed before work, or anticipated operating speed (mph).

**Citation:** Table 6B-4; Sec. 6B.08 ¶04, p. 775

### TAPER-02 — GUIDANCE — Taper type lengths merging L, shifting 0.5L, shoulder 0.33L, one-lane/downstream 50-100

Taper type lengths (Table 6B-3): merging at least L; shifting at least 0.5L (guidance: about 1/2 L); shoulder at least 0.33L (about 1/3 L); one-lane two-way taper 50-100 ft; downstream taper 50-100 ft.

**Citation:** Table 6B-3; Sec. 6B.08 ¶08,10, p. 775-776

### TAPER-03 — GUIDANCE — Downstream and one-lane taper device spacing ~20 ft

Downstream taper and one-lane two-way taper: devices at about 20 ft spacing.

**Citation:** Sec. 6B.08 ¶12,14, p. 776

### TAPER-04 — OPTION — Taper length adjustment near ramps/curves

Taper lengths may be adjusted near interchange ramps, crossroads, curves or other influencing factors. Longer tapers are not necessarily better, especially on urban streets with short blocks or driveways.

**Citation:** Sec. 6B.08 ¶01,03, p. 775

### TAPER-05 — GUIDANCE — Shoulder as travel lane uses merging/shifting taper

If a shoulder is used as a travel lane, a normal merging or shifting taper should be used instead of a shoulder taper.

**Citation:** Sec. 6B.08 ¶10, p. 776

### TAPER-06 — STANDARD — Near-side intersection taper not both lanes

Near-side intersection lane closure: the merging taper shall direct traffic into either the right or left lane, not both.

**Citation:** TA-21 note 1, p. 900

### BUFFER-01 — OPTION — Longitudinal buffer Table 6B-2 lengths

A longitudinal buffer space may be used in advance of the work space and may use Table 6B-2 stopping sight distance for its length: 20 mph 115 ft; 25 mph 155; 30 mph 200; 35 mph 250; 40 mph 305; 45 mph 360; 50 mph 425; 55 mph 495; 60 mph 570; 65 mph 645; 70 mph 730; 75 mph 820.

**Citation:** Sec. 6B.06 ¶09,11; Table 6B-2, p. 773, 775

### BUFFER-02 — GUIDANCE — No work/storage in buffer; lateral judgment

Neither work activity nor storage of equipment, vehicles or material should occur in a buffer space. Lateral buffer width: engineering judgment. When a shadow vehicle, arrow board or CMS is in a closed lane ahead of the work space, only the area upstream of it counts as buffer.

**Citation:** Sec. 6B.06 ¶07,13,15, p. 773, 775

### CHAN-01 — GUIDANCE — Device spacing taper 1S tangent 2S

Maximum spacing of cones, tubular markers, vertical panels, drums and barricades: taper channelization at most 1 x speed limit (mph) ft; tangent channelization at most 2 x speed limit (mph) ft.

**Citation:** Sec. 6K.01 ¶04, p. 815

### CHAN-02 — GUIDANCE — Extension 2S beyond transition

Where devices could lead traffic out of the intended path (Fig. 6P-39), extend them 2 x speed limit (mph) ft beyond the downstream end of the transition area.

**Citation:** Sec. 6K.01 ¶05, p. 815

### CHAN-03 — STANDARD — Cone heights 18/28 in

Cones: predominantly orange; at least 18 in tall for daytime/low-speed; at least 28 in on freeways/high-speed roads, at night on all highways, or where more conspicuous guidance is needed. At night cones shall be retroreflectorized or equipped with lighting devices.

**Citation:** Sec. 6K.03 ¶01-03, p. 818

### CHAN-04 — STANDARD — Tubular markers

Tubular markers: at least 18 in tall and 2 in wide facing traffic; at least 28 in on freeways/high-speed roads and at night. Should be used only where space does not allow more visible devices (Guidance). Must be attached to pavement to show the 2 in face.

**Citation:** Sec. 6K.04 ¶01-04,07, p. 819

### CHAN-05 — STANDARD — Vertical panels

Vertical panels: retroreflective striped material 8-12 in wide, at least 24 in tall, 45-degree stripes sloping down in the direction traffic is to pass.

**Citation:** Sec. 6K.05 ¶01, p. 819

### CHAN-06 — STANDARD — Drums

Drums: at least 36 in tall and 18 in wide; lightweight deformable materials; no metal drums; closed tops; alternating orange/white 4-6 in horizontal stripes (min two of each, top orange).

**Citation:** Sec. 6K.06 ¶01, p. 820

### CHAN-07 — STANDARD — Barricade rails

Barricade rails 8-12 in wide with 45-degree orange/white stripes (6 in, 4 in if rail under 36 in). Type 1 and 2 barricades at least 24 in long; Type 3 at least 48 in long. High-speed roads: at least 270 sq in retroreflective area facing traffic.

**Citation:** Sec. 6K.07 ¶03-05, p. 820

### CHAN-08 — GUIDANCE — Barricade type selection

Type 3 barricades should be used to close or partially close a road; Type 2 or 3 on freeways/expressways/high-speed roads. Type 1 may be used on conventional roads/urban streets (Option). Where legally closed but local access allowed, a sign with the permitted-use legend shall be installed (Standard).

**Citation:** Sec. 6K.07 ¶17-19,21-22, p. 821

### CHAN-09 — GUIDANCE — Direction Indicator Barricades

Direction Indicator Barricades (W1-6 arrow 24x12 in over striped rail 24x8 in) may be used in tapers/transitions; if used, should be in a series through the transition into the intended lane.

**Citation:** Sec. 6K.08 ¶01-04, p. 821

### CHAN-10 — GUIDANCE — Temporary barrier not for merging taper except low-speed urban

Temporary traffic barriers should not be used for a merging taper except in low-speed urban areas; when used for channelization they shall be supplemented with delineation/markings/devices (Standard) and are not to be used solely to channelize.

**Citation:** Sec. 6K.09 ¶03-06, p. 822

### CHAN-11 — STANDARD — Longitudinal channelizers / lane separators

Longitudinal channelizing devices and temporary lane separators are not crashworthy barriers and should not shield obstacles or protect pedestrians/workers. Lane separators: base max 4 in high and 1 ft wide; at pedestrian crossings they shall leave an opening at least 60 in wide.

**Citation:** Sec. 6K.10 ¶08; Sec. 6K.11 ¶02,05-06, p. 822-823

### CHAN-12 — GUIDANCE — Warning lights on devices

Warning lights on channelizing devices: maximum spacing same as the device spacing. Lights on devices used in a series shall be steady-burn; flashing only on isolated devices/clusters, except sequential flashing on a merging taper (upstream to downstream, 55-75 flashes/min). Minimum mounting height 30 in to lens bottom.

**Citation:** Sec. 6L.07 ¶06,09-12,14, p. 835

### PED-01 — GUIDANCE — Pedestrian path width 60 in; passing every 200 ft

Provide the width of the existing pedestrian facility if practical. If 60 in cannot be maintained the full length, provide a 60 x 60 in passing space at least every 200 ft. TA-28 option: alternate route may be 48 in wide with 60 in passing area every 200 ft.

**Citation:** Sec. 6K.07 ¶10; TA-28 note 14, p. 820, 914

### PED-02 — STANDARD — Sidewalk closure detectable devices

Sidewalk closure device shall cover the entire sidewalk width and be detectable by long-cane users: continuous detection plate (bottom at most 2 in, top at least 8 in above walkway) and hand-trailing edge (top 32-38 in above walkway).

**Citation:** Sec. 6K.02 ¶07-08, p. 817-818

### PED-03 — STANDARD — Temporary ramps 12:1

Temporary ramps 12:1 (8.33%) or flatter, slip-resistant, landing at least 48 x 48 in with 2% or flatter cross-slope.

**Citation:** TA-28 note 2, p. 914

### PED-04 — GUIDANCE — Direct pedestrians at intersections

Pedestrians should be directed at intersections rather than midblock (in high-volume urban/suburban areas) to cross to the open side; pedestrian signal displays for closed crosswalks should be covered or deactivated.

**Citation:** Sec. 6C.02 ¶09; TA-29 note 5, p. 778, 916

### PED-05 — GUIDANCE — Close crosswalk if work across it

If a work space extends across a crosswalk, close the crosswalk using the devices in Fig. 6P-29 (TA-29).

**Citation:** TA-21 note 3, p. 900

### SHLD-01 — STANDARD — Wide shoulder >=8 ft advance warning and channelization

When paved shoulders 8 ft or wider are closed: at least one advance warning sign shall be used, and channelizing devices shall close the shoulder in advance to mark the start of the work space and keep traffic in the traveled way (TA-3). TA-33 note 3 uses 'should' for the same 8 ft threshold on lane closures.

**Citation:** TA-3 note 8; TA-33 note 3, p. 864, 924

### ARROW-01 — GUIDANCE — Arrow mode for lane closure multi-lane

Arrow or chevron mode should advise of a lane closure on major multi-lane roads with heavy volume, high speed, and/or limited sight distance; use with signs and channelizing devices. Arrow board in arrow/chevron mode shall be used only for stationary or moving lane closures on multi-lane roadways (Standard).

**Citation:** Sec. 6L.06 ¶02-03,17, p. 832-833

### ARROW-02 — STANDARD — Arrow board per closed freeway lane

An arrow board shall be used when a freeway lane is closed; when more than one freeway lane is closed, a separate arrow board shall be used for each closed lane. More generally, for multiple lane closures a separate board per closed lane.

**Citation:** TA-33 note 7; TA-37 note 1; Sec. 6L.06 ¶21, p. 834, 924, 932

### ARROW-03 — STANDARD — Caution mode for shoulder/two-lane; no lane shift indication

Shoulder work, blocking the shoulder, roadside work near the shoulder, or closing one lane of a two-lane two-way road: arrow board only in caution mode. Arrow boards shall not indicate a lane shift and shall only indicate a lane closure.

**Citation:** Sec. 6L.06 ¶18,25, p. 833, 835

### ARROW-04 — GUIDANCE — Arrow board at beginning of merging taper

Stationary lane closure: arrow board on the shoulder at the beginning of the merging taper (TA-33 shows it at the start of the shoulder taper); if the shoulder is narrow, in the closed lane. Placed on the shoulder or farther from the traveled lane if practicable, delineated with retroreflective devices.

**Citation:** Sec. 6L.06 ¶04,19-20; Fig. 6P-33, p. 833, 925

### ARROW-05 — GUIDANCE — Multiple lane closure board placement

Multiple lane closures: if the first board is on the shoulder, put the second in the first closed lane at the upstream end of the second merging taper; if the first is in the closed lane, put the second in the second closed lane at the downstream end of the second merging taper. Exceptions (TA-37 note 2): use the interior lane at the downstream end of the second taper when the second board is on a shadow vehicle, when alignment could confuse which lane is closed, or when the first board is in the closed exterior lane.

**Citation:** Sec. 6L.06 ¶22; TA-37 note 2, p. 834, 932

### ARROW-06 — STANDARD — Arrow board types A-D sizes

Arrow board types (Fig. 6L-3): A min 48x24 in, 1/2 mile legibility, 12 elements (low-speed urban); B 60x30 in, 3/4 mile, 13 elements (intermediate speed; mobile/maintenance on high-speed); C 96x48 in, 1 mile, 15 elements (high-speed, high-volume projects); D arrow-shaped 48 in long, 1/2 mile, 12 elements (agency-authorized vehicles).

**Citation:** Fig. 6L-3; Sec. 6L.06 ¶05-06, p. 833-834

### ARROW-07 — GUIDANCE — Mounting height 7 ft; flash rate; vehicle lights

Minimum mounting height 7 ft to bottom of board (as high as practicable if vehicle-mounted); should be removed when not used, else shielded or delineated. A vehicle with an arrow board shall have high-intensity rotating/flashing/strobe lights (Standard). Flash rate 25-40 per minute; yellow elements; dimming at least 50% for night (Standard).

**Citation:** Sec. 6L.06 ¶04,09,11-15,24, p. 833, 835

### FLAG-01 — STANDARD — Flagger uses paddle/flag/AFAD; no hand alone

Flaggers shall use a STOP/SLOW paddle, flag, or AFAD to control traffic; hand movements alone are prohibited in one-lane two-way operations (exceptions: emergency responders at incident scenes, uniformed law enforcement). Paddle is the preferred device (Guidance, Sec. 6D.02).

**Citation:** Sec. 6D.05 ¶02; Sec. 6D.02 ¶01, p. 783, 785

### FLAG-02 — STANDARD — Flagger station stopping distance; Table 6B-2 may be used

Flagger stations shall be located so approaching road users have enough distance to stop at the intended stopping point; if sight distance cannot be achieved, location may be modified by engineering judgment. Table 6B-2 stopping sight distances may be used to locate the station and may be increased for downgrades and other conditions.

**Citation:** Sec. 6D.06 ¶01-03, p. 786

### FLAG-03 — GUIDANCE — Escape route; stand on shoulder; visible alone

Station should give an errant vehicle extra space to stop before the work space; flagger should have an identified escape route; stand on the shoulder or in the closed lane before stopping traffic, in the moving lane only after traffic has stopped; be visible to the first approaching driver; stand alone away from workers/equipment.

**Citation:** Sec. 6D.06 ¶04; Sec. 6D.05 ¶06, p. 786

### FLAG-04 — STANDARD — Advance warning and night illumination

Except in emergencies, flagger stations shall be preceded by advance warning sign(s) and shall be illuminated at night.

**Citation:** Sec. 6D.06 ¶05, p. 786

### FLAG-05 — GUIDANCE — One-lane two-way flagger each end / single option

Flagger at each end of a constricted section, one designated as coordinator, able to communicate. If the zone is short enough to see end to end, one flagger or one at each end is allowed (Option); a single flagger stands on the shoulder opposite the constriction/work space where visibility can be maintained, otherwise use a flagger at each end.

**Citation:** Sec. 6E.02 ¶01-03, p. 787

### FLAG-06 — GUIDANCE — Flag transfer max ~1 mile

Flag transfer method should be used only where one-way traffic is confined to a relatively short length, usually no more than 1 mile.

**Citation:** Sec. 6E.03 ¶02, p. 787

### FLAG-07 — GUIDANCE — TA-10 buffer before curve; sign order

TA-10: the buffer space should be extended so the one-lane two-way taper sits before a horizontal or crest vertical curve, giving the flagger and queue adequate sight distance. Taper: 50-100 ft (TAPER-02). Sign order upstream to downstream: W20-1, W20-4, W20-7 at spacings C, B, A (Table 6B-1); W20-7 (flagger) closest to the taper.

**Citation:** TA-10 note 6; Fig. 6P-10, p. 878-879

### FLAG-08 — STANDARD — AFAD placement and attendance

AFADs only where one lane of approaching traffic is controlled; placed in advance of one-lane two-way tapers and downstream of where traffic is to stop; two flaggers for Method 1 (AFAD each end) or Method 2 (AFAD one end, flagger the other) unless one flagger has unobstructed view of the AFAD(s) and traffic in both directions; AFAD never left unattended; illuminated at night; advance signing should include W20-1, W20-4 and W3-4; not for long-term stationary work (Guidance).

**Citation:** Sec. 6L.02 ¶04-06,09-15, p. 825-826

### FLAG-09 — STANDARD — STOP/SLOW AFAD 6 ft / 24x24

STOP/SLOW AFAD sign: octagonal, rigid, bottom at least 6 ft above the pavement, at least 24 x 24 in with letters at least 8 in high.

**Citation:** Sec. 6L.03 ¶02, p. 826

### FLAG-10 — STANDARD — TA-13 flagger or LEO for short closure

TA-13 (planned closure not exceeding 20 minutes, daytime): a flagger or uniformed law enforcement officer shall be used.

**Citation:** TA-13 note 1-2, p. 884
